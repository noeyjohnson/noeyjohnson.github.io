Expectations / Reality

This project explores the contrast between internal reality and external presentation, inspired by Jagged Little Pill and feminist themes of perfection, invisibility, identity, and voice.


Inspired by Jagged Little Pill, this project explores the pressure placed on women to appear “perfect” while hiding internal struggles, a concept reflected in The Beauty Myth. Through an intersectional lens, it also examines how identity shapes who is seen, heard, and understood. By contrasting private journal entries with curated external images, this project reveals the gap between what is performed and what is lived.

Themes to Focus on

  • Perfectionism
  • Intersectionality
  • How this connects to feminist themes
  • A tie to The Beauty Myth
  • Internal Struggle
